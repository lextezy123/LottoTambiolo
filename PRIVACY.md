# Privacy Policy for Lotto Tambiolo: Sim & Results

_Last updated: October 29, 2026_

Lotto Tambiolo: Sim & Results ("Tambiolo", "the app") is made by Lexteezy, an independent developer. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

## No account, no personal details
Tambiolo does not ask you to sign up, log in, or give your name, email, phone number, or location.

## Information stored only on your phone
- **Saved combos** (the names and numbers you save), your **language** and **theme** choices, and whether you bought **Remove ads** are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.
- The latest lotto results are downloaded from a public file and kept on your phone so they show offline. Downloading them does not send any personal information.

## Ads (Google AdMob)
The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:
- your device's **advertising ID** and similar device identifiers,
- your **approximate location** based on your IP address,
- **app interaction and diagnostic** information (for example, ad views, taps, and crash data).

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by Lexteezy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
Learn how Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Purchases (Google Play)
The optional one-time **Remove ads** purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. Lexteezy never sees your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children
Tambiolo is intended for users 18 years old and above and is not directed at children.

## No gambling
Tambiolo does not offer betting or real-money gambling. The draw simulator is for entertainment only. The app is not affiliated with the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office (PCSO), and results are for reference only.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
